Live data on the main page

To show current figures, your browser connects directly to these services when you open the main page, without any input from you: the public Cronos RPC (evm.cronos.org, fallback cronos-evm-rpc.publicnode.com) for the staked share and the public treasury wallet balances, and DEX Screener (api.dexscreener.com) for the current $CROFAM and CRO prices. Only technical data such as your IP address is transmitted. We do not receive or store it. The providers process it under their own privacy policies and may be located outside the EU or EEA. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing correct and current figures (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Wallet connection and "Your Lionhearts"

You can connect a crypto wallet that is installed in your browser. This only happens when you click "Connect wallet" and confirm in your wallet. The page then reads your public wallet address and uses the public Cronos RPC to look up which Lionhearts NFTs this address holds. It loads the NFT metadata and images from the address stored in the NFT contract, for example through an IPFS gateway (ipfs.io). Your address stays in your browser. We do not store it, and we never ask for your seed phrase or private key. The page does not send any transactions. If you choose WalletConnect, your browser loads the Reown AppKit library from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), and the connection to your mobile wallet runs through the relay servers of Reown (WalletConnect). Your IP address, your wallet address and technical connection data are processed by these providers under their own privacy policies and may be transferred outside the EU or EEA. This only happens when you click WalletConnect. The legal basis is your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in showing you your holdings and voting power (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

CROFAM polls

When you vote in a poll, your wallet signs a text message. We send this message and the signature to our vote server, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1). We store your public wallet address, your choice, the number of votes, the Lionhearts that were counted, the signed message and the time. To make the result verifiable, the wallet address, choice and number of votes of every vote are shown publicly in the record of each poll. Cloudflare also processes technical data such as your IP address and may process data outside the EU or EEA. The legal basis is your vote request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a transparent and fraud-resistant poll (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Votes are kept as long as the poll is shown on the website. You can ask us to delete your vote at any time.
